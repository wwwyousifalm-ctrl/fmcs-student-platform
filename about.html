About the Faculty & Student Association

Cultivating academic excellence, scientific research, and technological innovation at the University of Gezira.

🏛️ Our Faculty

The Faculty of Mathematical and Computer Sciences (FMCS) at the University of Gezira, located in Wad Madani, Sudan, is a leading academic institution offering premier undergraduate and postgraduate programs across Computer Science, Information Technology, Information Systems, and Applied Mathematics.

🎯 Association Mission

The FMCS Student Association bridges the gap between academic theory and industry practice. We empower students by organizing competitive hackathons, providing open academic resource repositories, hosting technical bootcamps, and representing student interests.
